Support

The fastest way to reach us is inside the app: Me → Write to the maker. Replies appear in that same screen.

How do I play?

Open a picture and tap the coordinate pairs in order. Lines form as you go, and closed areas fill with color. You only see the finished colors once you have plotted it.

How do I draw my own?

Studio → the plus button. Draw lines by tapping points or dragging, pour color inside a closed shape, and use the frame strip to animate. Pinch to zoom for precise work.

How do I print a worksheet?

Open a picture → share → File exports → Worksheet PDF. You get the puzzle page and an answer key. Set audience, paper size, hint dots, and answer key in Me → Worksheet.

Someone sent me a link. What do I do?

Open it. If you have Coordigo, the picture appears ready to plot. If you do not, the page shows the outline and a link to install the app.

What does Coordigo Studio unlock?

Unlimited worksheet PDFs, GIF and SVG export, storybook and web exports, removal of the small Coordigo mark on shared pictures, and bonus Dream credits. It is a one-time purchase — not a subscription.

I bought Studio but it is locked

Me → Restore Purchases, while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still does not unlock, write to the maker and we will sort it out.

How do I remove something I posted?

Me → Board → tap Delete next to the post, or use "Delete all my posts".

I saw something inappropriate on the board

Use the ⋯ menu on that post to report it or block its author. Reports are reviewed and posts are checked automatically before they appear.
