CoCoordigo Postcards

Postcards · Volume One

Someone walks through the picture you uncover.

These six are shot inside Coordigo landscapes — the same ones waiting under the grid in the app. A figure comes along the road, stops for a moment, and goes on. He passes behind the houses, because in these pictures every house and tree knows how far away it is.

He isn’t in the app. Nothing here is a feature — it is simply what these landscapes look like when someone is walking in them. The figure is hand-drawn in iFlipbook, our animation app, one frame at a time.
