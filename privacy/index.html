Privacy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Coordigo collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics in the app. What it remembers, it remembers on your device.

What never leaves your device

  • Which pictures you have opened and how far you have got in each one
  • Your settings, including whether music is on
  • The small counts the app keeps for itself — minutes traced, tiles finished, songs played or skipped — used only to choose what to offer you next
  • Anything you draw on a blank sheet

None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

iCloud

Your credit balance and the list of pictures you have opened are kept in Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, under your own Apple Account, so a second device of yours stays in step. That storage belongs to you; we cannot read it. Turning off iCloud for Coordigo keeps everything local.

Purchases

Credit packs are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether a purchase went through, so it can add the credits.

Songs and pictures

Songs — and any picture added after an update — are downloaded from our media server (media.coordigo.com, hosted on Cloudflare). Like any request on the web, that carries your IP address to the server; our host keeps short-term, standard delivery logs. We do not use them to identify anyone and do not join them with anything else. Once a file is downloaded it is cached on the device and not fetched again.

No profiles, of anyone

Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to profile, sell or share — for any user, of any age. We have no data to hand over and none to lose.

Removing your data

Delete the app: everything on the device goes with it. Credits and opened pictures live in your own iCloud storage and are removed with the app’s iCloud data in Settings. If you want anything else cleared, write to us.

Terms of use

Coordigo is covered by Apple’s standard licence agreement for apps.
