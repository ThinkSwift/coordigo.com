Coordigo

Privacy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Coordigo is a drawing and coordinate-plotting app. You can use almost all of it without sending anything to us: the pictures you plot, the drawings you make, and your progress stay on your device.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location
  • Advertising identifiers — Coordigo shows no ads and does no tracking
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera

We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

What stays on your device

Drawings you create, pictures you have plotted, your streak and stars, favorites and collections, and your app settings. Deleting the app removes them.

What is sent, and only when you choose it

An anonymous install ID

The features below attach a random identifier that is created on first launch and stored only on your device. It is not linked to you, your Apple Account, or any other app. Reinstalling the app creates a new one.

Dream (AI pictures and story text)

When you ask Coordigo to dream a picture or write a scene, your prompt is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns coordinates or text. Prompts are used to produce your result and to enforce rate limits — not to build a profile of you.

The board (posting a drawing)

If you post a drawing, that drawing, its title, and the handle you chose are stored in Apple's CloudKit public database so other people can see and plot it. Posts are checked automatically before they appear. You can delete any of your posts — or all of them at once — from the Me tab at any time.

Feedback

If you write to the maker, we receive your message along with the app version, build number, operating system, and language — so we can reproduce the problem. Nothing else.

Sharing a picture

When you share a picture as a link, the drawing itself is packed into the link, in the part after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so the picture travels directly to the person you sent it to. We do not store it and cannot see it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Coordigo asks Apple whether a purchase exists in order to unlock features.

Children

Coordigo is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The board shows drawings made by other people; it can be avoided entirely, and reporting and blocking are available on every post.

Removing your data

  • Posts: Me → Board → delete a post, or "Delete all my posts".
  • Everything on device: delete the app.
  • Anything else: write to us through Me → "Write to the maker" and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and the date at the top.
