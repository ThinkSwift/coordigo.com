How it works

Three rules, and that is the whole game.

1

Plot the pairs

Each picture is a list of ordered pairs. Tap them in order on a 15×15 grid.

2

Lines close

Your points join into lines. When a shape closes, it becomes an area.

3

Color pours in

Closed areas fill as you go. The picture appears under your finger.

More than a puzzle

Play it, draw it, print it, send it.

A grid editor with lines, fills, dots, and frame-by-frame animation. Make a picture, then make it move.

Printable worksheets

Share as a puzzle

Send a link and the picture travels with it. Your friend plots it to reveal the colors — no account, no upload.

Storybooks that move

Arrange your pictures into scenes with text, then read it as a picture book, a novel, or a blog post.

For teachers

Coordinate graphing practice that students actually finish, because the reward is a picture they made. Print a worksheet, hand it out, and the QR code brings it back to the app.
