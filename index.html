Read a coordinate.
Get a picture.

Coordigo gives you a list of ordered pairs and an empty grid. Tap them in order and the picture draws itself under your finger — you never see the colors until you plot it.

Plot the pairs

Tap each coordinate in order on a 15×15 grid.

Lines close

Points join into lines. A closed line becomes an area.

3

Color pours in

Each area fills as you finish it, so the picture grows as you work.

Then make your own

The puzzles are the doorway. What keeps people here is that everything they make leaves the app in a form someone else can use.

Draw it

A grid editor with lines, fills, and dots. Tap point by point or drag to sketch, then pour color into any closed shape.

Make it move

Add frames and the picture animates. Export a GIF and you have a short worth posting — the kind of clip that explains itself in two seconds.

Tell a story with it

Arrange your pictures into scenes and write your own words around them. Read it back as a picture book, or export it as a page you can post.

Send it as a puzzle

Share a link and the drawing travels inside it. Your friend sees the outline and plots it to reveal the colors. No account, no upload, nothing stored.

For teachers

Print a coordinate graphing worksheet — puzzle page and answer key, Letter or A4, sized for kids, adults, or a classroom. Every sheet carries a QR code, so the student who finishes it on paper can carry the picture back into the app.
